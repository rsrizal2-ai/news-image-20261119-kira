# Research notes — 19 November 2026

Researched on 19 November 2026 (morning UTC) by web search. Only stories
verified as happening or first reported on 19 November 2026, in some time
zone, were used. Several stories were decided or struck late on 18 November
in the Americas and reported on 19 November UTC / in Asia; their reporting
dates are noted below. Nothing was invented: every number and name on the
poster comes from the sources listed.

## Main stories

1. **Trump, Xi and Putin gather in Shenzhen.** The New York Times, 19 Nov
   00:57 UTC: "Trump, Xi and Putin Shake Hands as Leaders Gather in China".
   Xi Jinping hugged President Trump on the tarmac on arrival for the APEC
   summit in Shenzhen; the three leaders were due to hold a trilateral
   meeting on Thursday. Sources: NYT, Reuters, BBC.
2. **Crimean Bridge attacked a third time this week.** Reuters, 19 Nov
   00:36 UTC (reported by BBC 01:05 UTC): traffic was temporarily suspended
   over the bridge after an attack; Sergei Aksyonov, Crimea's
   Russian-installed governor, said on Telegram that damage assessment was
   under way. The bridge had already been hit twice earlier in the week,
   destroying the central road section. Sources: Reuters via BBC and
   syndication, 19 Nov.
3. **Jeffries nominated for Speaker.** House Democrats voted 251-11 in a
   closed-door ballot on Wednesday 18 November (US time) to nominate
   Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker of the House; the full House votes on
   3 January 2027. If elected, he would be the first Black Speaker in US
   history. Reported 19 Nov UTC by AP, CNN, NPR and USA Today.
4. **Cyclone Akvan nears India.** India Meteorological Department bulletin,
   18 Nov 13:37 IST: Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan over the Bay of Bengal,
   moving north-northwest, forecast to cross the North Andhra Pradesh &
   Yanam and South Odisha coasts between Machilipatnam and Puri by noon
   20 November as a severe cyclonic storm, sustained winds 90-100 km/h
   gusting to 110 km/h. Seven Andhra districts under red alert for
   19 November; Odisha identified more than 200,000 people for possible
   evacuation. Sources: IMD, QNC News, The Daily Jagran (18-19 Nov).
5. **ICJ delivers Myanmar genocide verdict.** The International Court of
   Justice is scheduled to deliver its judgment at 10:00 at the Peace
   Palace in The Hague on Thursday 19 November, in The Gambia v. Myanmar
   (Genocide Convention case over the 2017 crackdown on the Rohingya).
   The judgment's outcome was not yet known at research time (morning
   UTC), so the poster names the delivery of the verdict, not its result.
   The court has never found a state guilty of genocide since it began
   operating in 1946. Sources: ICJ notice via HRW / newsdeskreport
   (18 Nov), UN Special Rapporteur briefing (18 Nov).
6. **GTA VI launches worldwide.** AP, 19 Nov 02:13 UTC: "After a 13-year
   wait, 'Grand Theft Auto VI' arrives with enormous hype and
   expectations". The game returns to Vice City. Sources: AP via news
   roundups, 19 Nov.
